NEED TO KNOW?

Frequently asked questions.

Helpful answers to common questions about dental care.

Can medication completely relieve tooth pain?

Not entirely. While medications may offer short-term relief, lasting relief requires identifying and treating the root cause through professional dental care.

Does teeth scaling damage enamel or create gaps?

No. Scaling removes plaque and tartar. Temporary sensitivity may occur, but scaling does not damage healthy enamel or create gaps between teeth.

Can scaling remove fluorosis stains?

Scaling removes surface stains but not fluorosis, which is an internal stain. A dentist can discuss suitable cosmetic options.

Can painful teeth be treated with fillings?

Not always. If decay reaches the tooth pulp, root canal treatment or extraction may be considered after a dental examination.

Is a crown necessary after root canal treatment?

A crown is often recommended to protect a treated tooth, depending on how much tooth structure remains and the tooth's condition.

Can milk teeth need root canal treatment?

Yes. In some cases, treatment can preserve a badly decayed milk tooth and help prevent infection or premature tooth loss.

How can I maintain good oral hygiene?

Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste, clean between your teeth, rinse after meals when appropriate, and schedule regular dental check-ups.

What is the best age to get braces?

It depends on the individual. Many children are assessed for orthodontic treatment around ages 7–12, while braces may be fitted later depending on their needs.

Do all wisdom teeth need to be removed?

No. Removal may be recommended if wisdom teeth cause problems such as infection, impaction or damage to nearby teeth.

Should I replace a missing tooth after extraction?

Replacement may help restore chewing and appearance and maintain bite function. Ask your dentist which options suit your situation.

What are the benefits of dental implants?

Dental implants can replace missing teeth and support chewing function and appearance. Suitability depends on an individual's oral health.

Which toothbrush and toothpaste should I use?

Choose a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste. Replace your toothbrush regularly, typically every three months or sooner if worn.

What causes tooth sensitivity?

Possible causes include enamel wear, tooth decay, exposed roots and cracks. A dentist can help identify the cause.

How often should I visit a dentist?

Many people benefit from check-ups every six months, but your dentist may recommend a different schedule based on your oral health.

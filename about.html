OUR STORY

A journey of care.
A legacy of trust.

Sakthi Dental Clinic was founded by Dr. Anupriya in 2004 with a commitment to providing compassionate, dependable dental care to individuals and families in Hosur.

After graduating in 2000, Dr. Anupriya gained six years of clinical experience at Mathura Clinic before establishing Sakthi Dental Clinic.

She has also served as a consultant at PHC Chandara Hospital for over a decade, reflecting her continued commitment to patient care.

OUR SERVICES

Find the care you need.

Speak with our dental team to understand which treatment may be suitable for you.

01

Teeth Cleaning & Scaling

Professional cleaning helps remove plaque and hardened deposits that regular brushing may miss.

02

Tooth Filling

Dental fillings restore teeth affected by cavities or minor damage, following a dentist's assessment.

03

Tooth Extraction

A tooth may need removal when it cannot be predictably preserved. Your dentist will explain the available options.

04

Complete Dentures

Custom-made dentures can help replace a full set of missing teeth and support everyday oral function.

05

Dental Implants

Implants may replace missing teeth. Suitability depends on individual oral health and professional evaluation.

06

Laser Dentistry

Laser technology may be used for selected dental procedures when clinically appropriate.

07

Root Canal Treatment

This treatment addresses infection or damage inside a tooth with the aim of preserving it when possible.

08

Wisdom Tooth Removal

Assessment helps determine whether a wisdom tooth requires monitoring or removal.

09

Dental Bridges

A fixed bridge can replace one or more missing teeth when suitable for the patient's needs.

10

Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening options may help improve tooth colour after checking that your teeth and gums are healthy.

11

Dental Veneers

Thin coverings may improve the appearance of selected teeth following a consultation about benefits and limitations.

12

Pediatric Dentistry

Child-friendly dental visits support children's oral health and help build positive care habits.

13

Flap Surgery

Selected gum conditions may require surgical treatment following a periodontal assessment.

14

Orthodontic Braces

Braces gradually move teeth to improve alignment under professional orthodontic supervision.

15

Clear Aligners

Custom transparent aligners may help correct certain alignment concerns after an orthodontic evaluation.
